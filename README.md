SIMPLE SALES DASHBOARD DESIGN
Step 1: Load the Data into Power BI
Step 2: Understand and Clean the Data
	 PRODUCTLINE – Product category
	 COUNTRY – Region 
	 ORDERDATE – For month-wise analysis
	 SALES – The value you want to analyse. 
Step 3: Create a Month Column
	Created a month column using Orderdate and extracted month names.
Step 4: Create Visualizations
1.	Sales by Product
Visualization: Bar Chart
Axis: PRODUCTLINE
Values: SALES (Sum)
2.	Sales by Region
Visualization: Map or Bar Chart
Location: COUNTRY
Values: SALES (Sum)
3.	Sales by Month
Visualization: Line Chart
Axis: Month
Values: SALES (Sum)
Add slicers for filtering:
    Add a slicer for PRODUCTLINE
    Add a slicer for COUNTRY

  

1. "USA had the highest sales in Q3 2004, totaling $407,497."
 ->  This suggests strong market performance in that region during the late year.
 2. "Classic Cars generated peak sales of $452,924 in November 2003."
->  This product line had a standout performance in that specific month.
3. "Australia showed the highest average quarterly growth in sales."
->  Indicates consistent sales acceleration over time in the Australian market.
